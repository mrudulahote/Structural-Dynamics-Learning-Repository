# Structural-Dynamics-Learning-Repository
During my summer internship at Earthquake Department, IIT Roorkee i strengthened  the basics of structural dynamics including response of a single degree of freedom of a system with free vibration, single degree freedom system subject to harmonics force excitation forced vibrations using Duhamel’ Integral and non-homogenous forces using numerical techniques such as the central Difference method and Newmark’s Method have been studied. Also, earthquake damage parameters such as peak ground acceleration, peak ground velocity, and peak ground displacement. Arias intensity, earthquake power, the frequency content of an earthquake, Fourier amplitude spectrum, effective peak acceleration, and effective peak velocity by ATC, elastic spectrum intensity, characteristics intensity, frequency ratio, and medium period of an earthquake have also been studied. The simulation studies have been carried out using Python.
